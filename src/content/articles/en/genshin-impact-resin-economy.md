Every other resource in Genshin can be farmed, traded around or waited out. Resin cannot. It
regenerates at one unit every eight minutes — 180 a day — and caps at 200, which means the cap is
reached in about 26 hours of not playing. That single number decides how fast an account grows, and
it rewards planning far more than it rewards playing longer.

## Why resin is the real progression currency

Talent books, weapon ascension materials, boss drops for character ascension, artifacts: all of it
comes out of the same 180-a-day allowance. Mora and character XP do too, indirectly. Your account
does not grow because you played six hours; it grows because you spent 180 resin on the right thing.
Two players with wildly different playtimes and identical resin discipline end up in the same place.

## The spending order that works

For an account that is not yet at Adventure Rank 45, the order is not controversial and most players
still get it wrong:

1. **Character and weapon ascension bosses.** Ascension raises base stats and unlocks talent levels.
   Nothing else you can buy with resin moves damage as much per unit spent.
2. **Talent books, on their rotation days.** Talent levels are a flat multiplier on your main damage
   dealer. They are gated to specific weekdays, so they are the one cost you genuinely have to
   schedule.
3. **Weapon materials**, to get your carry's weapon to the same tier as the character.
4. **Artifacts, last.**

Artifacts go last not because they matter least — at the top end they matter most — but because their
return is random and their floor is high. A four-star artifact set with correct main stats beats a
mismatched five-star set, and you will pick those up incidentally long before it is worth farming
domains on purpose.

## Condensed Resin, and when not to use it

Condensed Resin turns 40 resin into one item that doubles a ley line or domain run. It saves loading
screens and halves the number of runs. Use it freely on artifact domains and ley line outcrops.

Do not use it on weekly bosses — they do not accept it — and do not craft a stack of it before you
know what you are farming this week. Condensed Resin sitting in your inventory is resin you have
already committed; if your priority changes on Tuesday, it is committed to the wrong thing.

## The two things worth overflowing for

Overflowing the cap wastes resin, and the usual advice is never to do it. There are two honest
exceptions. The first is a weekly boss you have not cleared: those drop talent materials that
nothing else does, and missing the weekly reset costs more than a few overflowed units. The second is
an event that pays out in the currency you are short of — event resin conversions are typically
better value than any domain.

## What to do with a day you cannot log in

Nothing, and that is the point. Genshin's daily loop is 26 hours long by design; a missed day is a
partial overflow, not a lost day. The players who burn out are the ones treating a 200-unit cap as an
obligation rather than a ceiling. Log in every second day, spend deliberately, and your account grows
at very nearly the same rate as the player who never misses.
