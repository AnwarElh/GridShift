ZZZ has been on Windows, iOS, Android and PS5 since July 2024, with Xbox Series added in June 2025.
Progress is carried by the account, so the platform decision is reversible. What is not reversible is
the server region, and what does not travel is everything about how the game feels to play.

## One account, five clients, one region choice

Your progress lives on a HoYoverse account bound to a server region. The client you use is free to
change and can change daily. The region cannot — there is no official transfer — so if you have
friends in the game, match their region before you play an hour you would mind losing.

## The platform question is a latency question

This matters more in ZZZ than in its stablemates, and it is worth being blunt about why. The
combat is built on frame-tight interrupts: a Dodge Counter requires dodging at the moment an attack
lands, and a Defensive Assist has a window measured in a handful of frames. Anything that adds
latency between your input and the game does not make ZZZ marginally worse; it removes a mechanic.

That rules out cloud play and wireless display setups for anyone playing above the mid difficulty
tiers, and it argues strongly for a wired controller or keyboard on PC and console.

## Settings worth changing on every device

Settings are stored per device, so a new install means configuring again. Four are worth doing
deliberately:

- **Frame rate.** Set the highest your hardware sustains, and prefer a stable 60 to a fluctuating
  higher number. The assist windows are read visually; a variable frame rate makes them inconsistent.
- **Camera assist / lock-on.** The default lock-on is aggressive and will pull your camera off the
  enemy that is about to throw the heavy attack. Turning it down is the most common fix for "I never
  see the assist prompt".
- **Controller vibration on PS5.** On by default and genuinely informative here — the parry has a
  distinct feel. Worth leaving on even if you disable it elsewhere.
- **Combat interface scale on mobile.** The default puts the assist prompt near where your thumb
  sits. Move it.

## Mobile: the honest battery position

ZZZ at 60 fps on a phone is a real version of the game and also a real drain on the battery, with the
thermal throttling that follows about thirty minutes in. Since the daily loop is capped by Battery
Charge anyway — one unit per six minutes, 240 maximum — mobile is well suited to the routine part of
the game and poorly suited to the hard content. Use it for dailies and events; do the difficult
fights somewhere plugged in.

## Which one to make primary

PC if you have one: highest frame rate, lowest latency, and the cleanest read on the assist prompts.
PS5 or Xbox Series if you want it on a television, which is where the animation work is best served.
Mobile as the second device rather than the first.

Because the account carries, none of this is a commitment. Start where you are, and move when the
content gets hard enough that the input path starts to matter.
