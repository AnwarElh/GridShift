*Grand Theft Auto VI* will not arrive everywhere at once. PlayStation Store listings across 63
countries and regions show the game unlocking at **midnight local time on November 19**, which
means New Zealand gets in first and the US is among the last major markets to start.

The listings were compiled by **Vice**, which converted every regional window, and picked up by
**GameSpot** on September 10. Neither amounts to an announcement: the times come from store pages,
**Rockstar** has not confirmed them, and GameSpot says it has asked the studio for comment.

## When it unlocks where you are

Midnight on November 19 in each region, converted to US time. Everything below falls on
November 18 unless marked:

- **New Zealand** — 3:00 AM PT / 6:00 AM ET
- **Australia** (east coast) — 5:00 AM PT / 8:00 AM ET
- **Japan and South Korea** — 7:00 AM PT / 10:00 AM ET
- **Thailand** — 9:00 AM PT / noon ET
- **India** — 10:30 AM PT / 1:30 PM ET
- **United Arab Emirates** — noon PT / 3:00 PM ET
- **France, Germany and the rest of CET** — 3:00 PM PT / 6:00 PM ET *(our conversion, see below)*
- **United Kingdom** — 4:00 PM PT / 7:00 PM ET
- **Brazil** — 7:00 PM PT / 10:00 PM ET
- **US and Canada** — 9:00 PM PT / midnight ET
- **Mexico** — 10:00 PM PT / 1:00 AM ET, November 19

Two details in that list are worth slowing down for. The US store does not roll midnight across
its own time zones: the listing reads **12 AM Eastern**, so the West Coast can start at 9 PM on the
18th instead of waiting three more hours. And Vice's table does not break out continental Europe —
the CET line is our arithmetic on the same midnight-local rule, not a listing anyone has published.

## The 16-hour figure is wrong

Some early reports, GameSpot's among them, put New Zealand's unlock at 5 AM PT and the wait for the
US at 16 hours. That is Sydney's time, not Auckland's. New Zealand moves to daylight saving on
September 27, so by November its midnight falls at 11:00 UTC — 3 AM Pacific, 6 AM Eastern — and the
gap to the US listing is **18 hours**, the figure Vice gives.

## The New Zealand trick costs a second copy

The obvious question is whether you can borrow New Zealand's head start. Per Vice, the unlock
follows the region of your **PlayStation Network account**, not where the console sits — and
PlayStation does not let you change the region of an existing account.

So the method is a second account registered to New Zealand, with a second copy bought from that
store, paid for with a New Zealand card or New Zealand store credit, at New Zealand pricing. That is
a whole extra purchase to buy back 18 hours.

None of this covers Xbox. Every time above comes from the PlayStation Store, and none of the outlets
reporting them has published Xbox Store times.

## Before the 19th

Preloading opens on **November 12**, the same day the physical edition goes on sale — which,
according to GameSpot, is a code in a box, and does not unlock any earlier than the download.

If you are in the US and care about the story, November 18 is the day to mute the game's name.
Auckland will have been playing since before your morning coffee.
