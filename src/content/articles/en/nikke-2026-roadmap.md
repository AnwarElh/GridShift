Shift Up has laid out a 2026 roadmap for Goddess of Victory: NIKKE running through October, and the
notable parts are what is being reduced rather than what is being added.

## Fewer crossovers, deliberately

Only two external IPs will appear in the game across the year. The studio's stated reason is to give
more focus to NIKKE's own story — a pointed decision for a game whose crossovers have been among its
most visible moments. Whether players read that as discipline or as a thinner year is likely to
depend on which two land.

## Surface moves back

The Surface content push has been postponed, with updates instead spread across the rest of 2026 and
into 2027. That is the roadmap item most likely to frustrate long-term players, and the studio has
not tied it to a specific window.

## What is scheduled

The roadmap covers the SSR character Velvet and the "Maid for You" event, a main story update and a
Solo Raid Museum Boss update in February, and a fourth-anniversary main story and event story update
in November. Shift Up also says it intends to keep tightening how characters, battles, animation,
music and narrative fit together — the kind of statement that is only measurable in retrospect.
