With the August 2026 console launch, Once Human runs on Windows, iOS, Android, PlayStation 5 and Xbox
Series X|S, with cross-play and cross-progression across them. That changes the install decision from
a commitment into a preference, but there are details worth setting up deliberately.

## Progress is shared; input is not

Cross-progression means your account, not your settings. Control schemes, sensitivity and the new
first-person toggle are things you will configure per device. The first-person option added at the
console launch is a genuine perspective switch rather than a camera preset, and it is worth
configuring on each platform separately — what reads well on a television at controller distance is
not what reads well on a phone.

## Pick a primary anyway

Cross-progression removes the penalty for playing on a second device, but a survival game with base
building rewards a primary platform. Base layout work is fiddly on a touchscreen and comfortable with
a mouse; combat is the reverse for many players. The workable split is building on PC and playing
sessions elsewhere.

## Xbox specifics

The game arrived day one on Game Pass with launch perks attached. That does not change the free-to-
play structure — the game is free regardless — but it does affect where console players are likely to
discover it, and therefore which platform your friends are on.

## PlayStation specifics

PS5 has DualSense-specific immersion features. Whether that argues for playing there is a matter of
taste; it is the only platform-exclusive treatment in the list.
