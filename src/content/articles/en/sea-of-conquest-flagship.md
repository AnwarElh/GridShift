Sea of Conquest presents itself as a pirate adventure in the Devil's Seas. Structurally it is an
empire builder in the mould of Evony or Age of Origins, with one real difference: your base is a ship
and it moves. Once you see it that way the priorities sort themselves out.

## Upgrade the flagship before anything else

Your flagship carries the most power in the fleet, side ships follow it, and — the part that matters —
it is the only ship where cabins can be built and upgraded. Everything else is downstream of it.
Expect it to consume most of your gold.

## Cabins are buildings

Upgrading the ship means building rooms: armoury, henhouse, stable, refectory, galley. Each grants
bonuses or unlocks features, and flagship upgrades have cabin requirements attached, so a neglected
cabin blocks the whole ship. Read the requirement list before you spend.

## Put officers in the right chairs

Each ship has three officer positions — captain, first mate and gunner — unlocking as the ship
levels. Heroes are specialised for those roles, and placing a hero in its matching position grants an
additional 20% stat boost. A correctly seated mediocre hero beats a strong one in the wrong chair.

## Watch two resources, not one

Sailing consumes sailors and supplies. Sailors crew the decks and rooms; supplies keep them working,
which affects both sailing speed and damage. Running either dry does not stop you dramatically — it
just makes everything quietly worse until you notice.
