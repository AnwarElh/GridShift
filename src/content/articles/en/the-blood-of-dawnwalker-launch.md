The Blood of Dawnwalker is out today on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PC via Steam, the Xbox app
and GOG. It is the first game from **Rebel Wolves**, the Warsaw studio founded in February 2022 by
Konrad Tomaszkiewicz — director of The Witcher 3 and head of production on Cyberpunk 2077 — and it is
published by Bandai Namco. The game went gold in mid-July.

## The frame rate story, in order

Rebel Wolves announced that the console versions would be capped at **40 FPS**. The reaction was
immediate and loud, and it did not fade the way these things usually do.

On **August 24** the studio changed position. "We heard your feedback," it said, committing to a
**60 FPS mode delivered in a day-one patch**. Xbox Series S is excluded.

That is a climbdown, and it is worth being clear about which kind. A 40fps target on a UE5 open-world
RPG is a defensible engineering choice — it is what a studio picks when it wants a visual ceiling it
cannot hold at 60. Shipping a 60fps mode nine days after saying otherwise means one of two things:
either the mode existed and was not going to be exposed, or it was built under deadline pressure
created by a public argument. Rebel Wolves has not said which, and until people have hours on the
patched build nobody outside the studio can tell you what the 60fps mode costs elsewhere.

Our review was written largely on the 40fps build and re-tested after the patch.

## What the game is

A gothic RPG set in **Vale Sangora**, a 14th-century Carpathian kingdom assembled from myth,
archaeological finds and real structures in Hungary and Romania. A plague is spreading; the king has
ordered every infected person killed. The ancient vampire **Brencis** takes the kingdom, abolishes
taxes, and offers undead blood as a cure — for a **blood tax**, a few drops from every adult at a
monthly gathering where the weak and infirm are usually killed anyway.

**Coen**'s forced turning fails. He becomes a **Dawnwalker**, half-human and half-vampire, and
Brencis believes him dead. Then Brencis abducts Coen's family to be sacrificed at his investiture as
Prince — in one month.

That month is the game. **Thirty in-game days and nights, each split into eight time segments.** Only
plot-relevant actions spend them: quests and levelling. Exploring the world costs nothing, there is
no real-time timer, and an hourglass shows the price of an activity before you commit. There are
deliberately more time-consuming quests than segments available, so no run sees everything.

Human by day, with dialogue, non-violent solutions and witch magic that questions the recently dead.
Vampire by night, with wall-walking, a Shadowstep teleport and claws. It runs 50 to 70 hours.

## The reviews, and a wide spread

The embargo lifted on August 31. Destructoid's headline called it **the best game of 2026**. VGC
framed it as Witcher 3 veterans proving that a focused RPG beats a bloated one. PCGamesN went with
"Time well spent". Game Informer: "Time doesn't heal all wounds". Shacknews: "Protect ya neck".

GamesRadar is the dissent, and it is a specific one: despite clear Witcher 3 DNA, they wished this
vampire RPG were messier.

## Three editions, and a saga

Standard, the **Eclipse Edition** (soundtrack and a digital comic) and a **Collector's Edition**
(physical versions of both, a world map, a figure). There are two PC builds carrying different age
ratings by region.

The Summer Game Fest 2026 trailer showed Coen in a modern, 21st-century setting — Rebel Wolves is
teasing a saga across eras. The studio has said each entry will be self-contained: no cliffhangers,
no unresolved threads, and no time travel.
