Eight years in, the opening advice for Rise of Kingdoms has settled — and the part most new players
get wrong is not which civilization to pick, but how long to keep it.

## China, for the building phase

China's 5% building speed cuts the wait on City Hall, Academy, Alliance Centre and the military
buildings, which is where almost all of your early time goes. The 5% action point recovery improves
barbarian farming on top. It also unlocks Sun Tzu, the most practical early commander in the game —
area damage, rage generation, and he works with infantry.

If you cannot take China, Britain is the next best opening.

## Then leave

This is the part that gets skipped. Building speed stops being valuable once the major construction
is behind you. China is the strongest *starting* civilization, not the permanent one. Once your
account has finished its building phase, Germany, France or Korea will usually pay better.

## Commanders worth your early investment

Sun Tzu first. Then Joan of Arc, Aethelflaed, Boudica and Scipio Africanus as practical options.
Aethelflaed deserves a specific mention: she is the only free-to-play legendary in the game,
obtainable from the medal store, and she is genuinely strong as a debuffer rather than a consolation
prize.

## The decision that outweighs all of the above

Join an active alliance early. Alliance help, shared technology, territory bonuses, gifts, rallies
and coordinated events compound in a way no civilization bonus matches. A mediocre commander roster
in a good alliance outperforms a good roster in a dead one.
