District one holds eleven optional areas. Four of them close permanently once you step through the
district two gate, without the slightest warning.

This guide was verified on version 4.1. Patch 4.2a changed the loot in two areas; we are re-running
it this week.

## The four areas that close

The drowned relay, the site shed, the underpass and the dispensary cellar. All four are reachable
as soon as you have the first traversal ability.
