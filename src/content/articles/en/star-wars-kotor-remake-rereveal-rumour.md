There is one substantial claim circulating about the Knights of the Old Republic remake, and it did
not come from Saber Interactive. An insider account operating as **"100% Star Wars"** says the game
is close to being shown again. Here is the claim, and here is why we are not treating it as news
about the game itself.

## The claim

Three parts, as reported:

1. Development has **"not only been ramping up, but it's going extremely smoothly over the past 3
   years since Saber started from scratch."**
2. A **re-reveal before the end of 2026** is the plan.
3. The venues in the frame are **The Game Awards**, a PlayStation **State of Play**, or a publisher
   showcase.

There is also a stated fallback: if the year runs out, the re-reveal slides to **early 2027**, timed
to coincide with **Star Wars Celebration**.

## Why the shape of it is plausible

Nothing in that is impossible, and one part of it is close to obvious. A game that has been silent
since a halt in 2022 has to reappear somewhere, and the list of places a Star Wars game can
plausibly reappear in a given year is short. The Game Awards, a State of Play and a publisher
showcase are not insider knowledge; they are the three doors that exist. Naming them costs a source
nothing and can never be wrong.

The "extremely smoothly" line is the part that would be real information if it were verifiable. It
is also the part that is least falsifiable from outside — nobody can check it, and nobody will be
able to check it until the game is either shown or is not.

## Four reasons to hold it loosely

**It is a single unverified source.** Saber has not confirmed it. Neither has Lucasfilm Games,
Embracer, or any platform holder. One account is one account, regardless of its track record.

**A re-reveal window is the easiest prediction in the industry to make and the hardest to be
penalised for.** "Before the end of the year, and if not, early next year at Celebration" covers
roughly six months of calendar with a built-in excuse attached. A prediction that cannot fail is
not a prediction.

**Marketing timing is rarely the developer's to leak.** The decision to show a Star Wars game
belongs to the licence holder and the publisher as much as to the studio. Studio-side confidence
about a build being in good shape and a scheduled reveal slot are different things, and the second
one is decided further up.

**It sits awkwardly against the only official numbers we have.** In July 2026 Saber's studio head
gestured at a release year, and a statement referenced the remake "hopefully" being in the lineup
for **2028**. A re-reveal in late 2026 for a 2028 game would mean roughly two years of marketing
runway. That is not unheard of — the original announcement in September 2021 was itself years ahead
of anything — but it does mean a 2026 re-reveal would tell you the game exists, and very little
about when you would play it.

## What Saber has actually said

For contrast, the official record for 2026 in full: CCO Tim Willits, in March, said **"Yes, it is
still in development. That's all I can say."** Saber's CEO said the game is **"alive and well."**
That is the whole thing. No date, no window, no platforms, no footage.

## What would change this

A calendar entry. If a showcase is announced and Saber or Lucasfilm Games confirms a presence, the
rumour becomes a schedule and we will write it up as one. Until then the only verified facts about
this game are that it was announced in 2021, halted in 2022, restarted under a new studio, and is
still being made.

We would rather publish that short list than a long article built on someone else's guess.
