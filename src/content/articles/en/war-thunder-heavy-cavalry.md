Gaijin shipped Heavy Cavalry in June 2026, and it is a substantial one: new vehicles across every
nation, graphical upgrades and gameplay changes. The headline additions run from the Welkin and the
IA.58A Pucará to newer Abrams variants and the USS North Carolina.

## Breadth across all three domains

What distinguishes a War Thunder major update from most live-service content drops is that it has to
serve air, ground and naval simultaneously. Heavy Cavalry does, which is also why the top-tier
additions draw the most attention: modern main battle tanks change the highest ranks more visibly
than a mid-tier aircraft does.

## The seasonal update alongside it

The Thunder Blossom seasonal update added the Japanese research branch to the tank campaign, the
research ship Niedersachsen to the German tree, and blueprints for top-tier vehicles.

## The part that does not change

War Thunder's economy has been its most argued-about system for years, and a content update does not
address that. New vehicles at top rank raise the same question they always do: what the grind to
reach them costs, and whether repair costs at that rank make playing them viable without premium.
That conversation will outlast this update as it has outlasted the others.

Current-generation consoles run the game at 4K and 60 fps, with cross-platform play across PC, Linux,
Mac, PlayStation and Xbox.
