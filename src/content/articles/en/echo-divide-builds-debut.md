An Echo Divide build is judged on a single question: does it hold up to district three, where the
respec tokens appear? Here are three that do.

## Endurance mender

The safest solo option. Put all the prologue currency into stamina up to 45, then into tools.
Self-healing covers your mistakes, stamina covers the travel.

## Traversal scout

The fastest and the most fragile. It goes for the second traversal ability in two hours instead of
six, at the cost of a very thin survival margin in district one.

## Component artificer

The most profitable long term, unplayable for the first three hours. Only take it in co-op.
