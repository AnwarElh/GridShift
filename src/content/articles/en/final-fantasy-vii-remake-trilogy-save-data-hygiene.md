Two of the games on this site's slate will read your old saves, and one of them will do it in a way
that punishes a decision you might make this month without thinking about it. This is a short,
boring guide to not losing something free.

## The one case with a confirmed reward

**Final Fantasy VII Revelation**, arriving 8 April 2027, grants early-game bonuses based on save data
from the earlier games:

| Save file | Unlocks in Revelation |
|---|---|
| **Remake Intergrade** | The "Chocobo and Moogle" Summon Materia set |
| **Rebirth** | The Phoenix Summon Materia, available earlier than it otherwise would be |

Set expectations correctly: these are **summon materia, not story content**. They are conveniences,
not decisive advantages, and nobody should restructure a year around them. But they cost nothing to
preserve, and deleting a save to reclaim space is the kind of thing people do in March without
remembering there was a reason not to.

## The platform trap that voids it

This is the part worth acting on.

The bonus reads save data **on the platform you play Revelation on**. A *Rebirth* save sitting on a
PS5 does nothing for a copy of *Revelation* running on Switch 2.

That matters more than it used to, because the trilogy stopped being a PlayStation project during
2026. *Remake Intergrade* and *Rebirth* both reached **Switch 2 and Xbox Series X|S**, with *Rebirth*
landing on Switch 2 on **3 June**. A lot of people now have the option to move — and moving is
exactly what breaks the link.

So the order of operations is:

1. **Decide where you will play Revelation** — PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2 or Windows.
2. **Make sure a save from the earlier games exists there**, which for most destinations is now
   possible.
3. Only then worry about anything else.

One further wrinkle for PC buyers: **Play Anywhere cross-progression exists only between the
Microsoft Store version and Xbox Series X|S**. There is no cross-progression between PlayStation and
anything, between Switch 2 and anything, or between Steam and a console. If your trilogy is going to
span a PC and a console, those are the two it can span.

## The Witcher 3's unanswered question

**CD PROJEKT RED has made no statement about save compatibility** between the current build and
*Remastered*, arriving 29 September.

That would be a minor gap except for one detail on the feature list: the remaster includes a **skill
tree revamp**. A change to how skills are structured is precisely the kind of thing that complicates
an in-progress character — and CDPR has said nothing about how existing saves are handled.

The safe posture, until they do:

- **Do not delete Witcher 3 saves.** They cost almost nothing to keep.
- **Do not start a fresh playthrough this month** if you can wait three weeks. Beginning a
  two-hundred-hour run immediately before a combat and skill overhaul is a poor use of the two
  hundred hours.
- **Claim Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine now** — they are already free for every current owner —
  but consider not starting them until the 29th.

## A ten-minute filing routine

Nothing clever, and it covers every case above:

1. **List what you own and where.** Which storefront, which console, which account. This single fact
   settles most of the decisions on this page.
2. **Do not delete saves for the games with sequels coming.** *Remake Intergrade*, *Rebirth*, *The
   Witcher 3*.
3. **If you are moving platforms, move before you need the save**, not in the week the sequel lands.
4. **Check cloud saves are actually enabled** on the platforms holding the files you care about.
   Local-only saves die with hardware.
5. **Write down which account owns what** if you use more than one. This is the most common way
   people lose access to a licence they paid for.

## What is not confirmed, stated plainly

To be explicit about the gaps rather than filling them:

- **Whether Witcher 3 saves carry into Remastered.** Unaddressed.
- **Whether the 2019 Switch version confers anything** toward the native Switch 2 release.
  Unaddressed.
- **Whether PS4 and Xbox One owners qualify** for the free Witcher 3 upgrade. CDPR's wording covers
  PS5 and Xbox Series X|S; the older generation is not mentioned either way.
- **Whether FF7 save detection has any cross-platform allowance.** Square Enix has not published the
  mechanism, only the bonuses.

We will update this guide as those are answered. Until then, keeping the files costs nothing and
assuming a transfer exists costs a summon materia and possibly a playthrough.
