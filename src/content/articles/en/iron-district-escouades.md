Version 2.1's deterministic cover changes everything: compositions built on volume of fire are now
worse than the ones built on positioning.

## The benchmark composition

One scout, two riflemen, one engineer. The engineer lays the mobile cover, the riflemen hold the
angle, the scout opens the line of sight. It finishes the campaign on veteran without particular
difficulty.
