HoYoverse has confirmed a crossover between Zenless Zone Zero and Honkai: Star Rail for winter 2026.
What it has not confirmed is a date, a character list, or whether the crossing goes both ways.

## Why this is unusual

HoYoverse has historically kept its games apart. Genshin, Star Rail and ZZZ share a house style, a
release cadence and a gacha structure, but not characters — each has been allowed to hold its own
canon. Crossovers in this space are normally licensing deals with an outside property, which makes an
internal one a different kind of decision: there is no rights-holder to satisfy, only two design
teams with incompatible combat systems.

That is the substance of the question. Star Rail is turn-based; ZZZ is a real-time action game built
on interrupts. A character cannot simply move across. Whatever arrives will be a reinterpretation,
and how faithful it is will tell you a good deal about how seriously HoYoverse takes the idea.

## What it means if you are playing now

Not much yet, which is the useful part. Winter is a season away, and a crossover of unknown scope is
not something to save pulls against — you do not know how many banners it involves or whether the
characters are limited at all. The 3.x cycle's permanent content, including Enigma of the Labyrinth,
is where account progress compounds in the meantime.

If you are returning specifically for the crossover, you have time to be comfortably established
before it lands. That is worth more than the currency you would have hoarded.
