Ragnarok Online: World Journey launched on 15 January 2026 across China, Hong Kong, Macau and
Taiwan, developed by 37 Interactive Entertainment and published by Gravity Game Vision. It runs
cross-platform on PC, iOS and Android on a global server architecture, and topped App Store
pre-order charts in all three of China, Hong Kong and Taiwan before release.

## The March update did the work

The game's first major version update arrived on 26 March 2026 and is the more interesting event of
the two. It opened **free job changes**, added the **Crestham Ancient City** map, introduced
**King's Battle** as a new PvP mode, and added the **Crusader** and **Monk** second-job branches.

Free job changing is the notable one. In a Ragnarok game, class choice has traditionally been a
long-term commitment that shapes an entire character; removing the cost of changing it two months
after launch is a decision about player retention, and a fairly aggressive one.

## Not available in Europe

World Journey has not launched in Western markets, which is worth stating plainly given how many
Ragnarok titles Gravity currently has in flight — the company announced four more at its Gravity
Visionary 2026 event, and launched Ragnarok: The New World in Southeast Asia in July.
