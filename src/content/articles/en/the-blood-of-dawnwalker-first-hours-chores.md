**Kotaku** published a piece on September 4 covering its first seven hours with *The Blood of
Dawnwalker* in an unusual format: staged as Anne Rice's vampires — Daniel Molloy, Armand, Louis,
Lestat and Claudia — sitting around the writer's couch commenting on the game as it loads.

It is a bit, and it is played as one. It also happens to isolate the sharpest available criticism
of *Dawnwalker*'s opening better than a straight write-up would have.

## The complaint underneath the joke

The line that does the work is Daniel's, delivered while the game has him **chopping wood and
looking for lost pigs**: is this whole thing *Peasant Simulator 3000*?

That is a real objection dressed as a gag. *Dawnwalker* sells a specific fantasy — a half-human,
half-vampire Dawnwalker with wall-walking, a Shadowstep teleport, and claws, moving through a
kingdom under a blood tax. The opening hours reportedly hand you agricultural errands first.

There is a defensible design reason for that. The thirty-day clock only advances on plot-relevant
actions, so an unhurried, low-stakes opening is where a player learns the systems without
spending the resource the whole game is about. But a tutorial that withholds the premise for
hours is a real cost, and it is the second complaint this week — after the [story timer
mod](/news/the-blood-of-dawnwalker-story-timer-mod/) — that comes down to players wanting the
game to get out of the way.

## What the format actually surfaced about the fiction

Where the piece turns complimentary is on the vampire lore, and specifically the turning ritual.

In *Dawnwalker*, the **vrakhiri** make another vampire by tearing out one of their own fangs and
driving it into a human's heart. Kotaku's Claudia calls it intentional and violent, and approves.
It is a good observation: most vampire fiction makes turning a byproduct of feeding, an accident
of appetite. Rebel Wolves made it a deliberate act that costs the maker something physical.

The piece also lands on the day-to-night transition and the **Blood Mass**, and on the pull to
drain someone mid-conversation — which, per the staged Lestat, is the part the game gets right.

## Worth reading, with the caveat in the title

Seven hours is seven hours. This is a first-impressions piece in costume, not a review, and it
does not claim otherwise. Our own review covers the full game.

What it is useful for is deciding whether *Dawnwalker*'s opening will test your patience. If
"chopping wood before I get to be a vampire" sounds like a dealbreaker rather than a warm-up, that
is a genuine signal, and it is the same friction the most-downloaded mod on Nexus exists to sand
off.
