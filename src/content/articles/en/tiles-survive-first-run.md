Most tile-matching games hand you a numbered ladder of levels and ask you to climb it. Tiles Survive!
puts a survival progression on top of the matching loop instead, which is the one thing worth
understanding before you start spending.

## The loop is familiar, the framing is not

The matching itself will be immediately legible to anyone who has played the genre. What differs is
what matching is *for*: progress is framed as survival rather than level completion, so the question
each board asks is whether you can sustain, not whether you can clear.

## Play for sustainability, not speed

That reframing has a practical consequence. In a level-ladder game, the fastest clear is the best
clear. In a survival frame, a board finished with resources intact is worth more than one finished a
few moves quicker but stripped bare. Spend as if the next board is the one that matters, because it
is.

## Free-to-play, so the constraint is patience

FunPlus ships this free with in-app purchases, on iOS and Android. The usual reading applies: the
paid shortcut exists, and the game is designed to be completable without it at a slower pace. Decide
which of the two you are playing before the first offer appears rather than after.
