Genshin's platform story is better than its reputation and worse than its competitors'. Progress does
travel, but on terms that are worth understanding before you install anything, because one of the
steps is irreversible.

## The account model, in one paragraph

Your progress lives on a HoYoverse account, not on a device, and that account is bound to a server
region — America, Europe, Asia or TW/HK/MO. The platform you play on is free to change. The region
is not: there is no official region transfer, so a character created on Europe stays on Europe
forever. Pick the region your friends are on, because co-op does not cross it.

## Linking a PlayStation account is permanent

PlayStation is the special case. A PSN account can be linked to a HoYoverse account so that one save
is shared between PS5 and PC or mobile — but the link is one-way and permanent. Once a PSN account
is bound, it cannot be unbound or re-bound to a different HoYoverse account.

The order matters. If you have an existing PC or mobile account you care about, link *that* one to
PSN. If you start fresh on PS5 and link later, the PlayStation progress is what survives. Getting
this backwards is the single most common way players end up with two accounts and neither of them
complete.

## The Steam Deck answer is no

Genshin is not officially supported on Steam Deck, and the reason is structural rather than
performance: the game's kernel-level anti-cheat does not run under Proton. There is no supported path
and no setting that changes it. If a portable Genshin is what you want, the answer is the mobile
client on a phone or tablet — it is the same account, and on current hardware it is a genuinely good
version of the game.

There is no Xbox release either. The platform list is PC, PlayStation, iOS and Android, and it has
been stable for years.

## What does not carry across devices

The account carries. Settings do not. Graphics presets, control bindings, controller layout and
touch-control positions are stored per device, so a fresh install means reconfiguring. Two settings
are worth doing deliberately on each platform rather than accepting the default:

- **Frame rate on mobile.** The client defaults conservatively. If your phone can hold 60, set it,
  and accept the battery cost — Genshin's dodge timing is noticeably easier to read at 60.
- **Camera speed on controller.** The console default is slow enough that aimed shots feel sluggish.
  Raise it before you judge the ranged characters.

## Which platform to make primary

Cross-save removes the penalty for switching, so the question is only where you spend most of your
hours. PC is the version with the highest ceiling and the only one where the overworld runs uncapped
in practice. PS5 is the most comfortable on a television and the only one with any platform-specific
treatment. Mobile is the one that makes the daily loop take four minutes instead of fifteen — which,
given that the daily loop is resin-capped anyway, is a stronger argument than it sounds.
