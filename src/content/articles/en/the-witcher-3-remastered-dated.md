**The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Remastered** launches on **September 29, 2026**, and for anyone who
already owns the game on a qualifying platform it costs **nothing**.

CD PROJEKT RED announced it at **gamescom Opening Night Live**, alongside a first look at a new paid
expansion, a new console, a new storefront, and a giveaway that took effect the moment the words left
the stage.

## The free upgrade, and who qualifies

*Remastered* is a **free upgrade** for existing owners of *The Witcher 3* on **PS5, Xbox Series X|S,
Steam, GOG and the Epic Games Store**. No repurchase, no upgrade fee.

That is worth pausing on, because it is not what the market has trained people to expect. This is a
2015 game that has sold **more than 65 million copies** and won **over 250 game of the year awards**,
being substantially reworked and handed to its existing audience at no charge, in a year when
remasters routinely ship at full price.

## Two new platforms

*The Witcher 3* is arriving in two places it has never been:

- **A native Nintendo Switch 2 version.** The game reached the original Switch in 2019 as a cloud-
  assisted port with well-documented compromises; this is native, and it is the first time.
- **Battle.net**, through a new partnership between CD PROJEKT RED and **Blizzard Entertainment** —
  described by CDPR as "a collaboration three decades in the making". Buying the forthcoming
  expansion on Blizzard's platform unlocks a **Geralt-inspired skin in Diablo IV**, with more due at
  **BlizzCon in September**.

Both land on September 29 alongside the PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S versions.

## The giveaway that happened on stage

Announced by gamescom host Geoff Keighley and effective immediately: **every current owner of The
Witcher 3 now has Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine at no cost.**

Those are the game's two acclaimed expansions, previously paid, and they are now simply part of what
you own. If you bought the base game at any point in the last eleven years and never picked up the
expansions, they are in your library.

## Songs of the Past — the paid part, in 2027

The other half of the announcement is a **new expansion**, and it is not free.

**Songs of the Past** launches in **2027** on PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S and Switch 2, and is available
to wishlist now. It takes Geralt to **Letten**, a brand-new region CDPR describes as "an idyllic land
of rural tradition that hides a dark secret just below the surface" — rich in folklore, bountiful
harvests and lavish festivals, with a long-dormant evil underneath.

Geralt gets **new abilities and a new weapon, the chain**, faces enemies the series has not used
before, and reunites with old friends. No release window narrower than "2027" has been given.

It is being **co-developed with Fool's Theory**, a studio whose leadership is made up of *Witcher 3*
veterans. *Remastered* itself is co-developed with **Yigsoft**, a team CDPR says has "a proven track
record working on The Witcher 3 already".

## What the remaster changes

CDPR's own list, which is long and which nobody outside the studio has yet tested:

**Visuals** — enhanced path tracing, updated dynamic lighting, higher-resolution textures, improved
environmental foliage across all regions.

**Combat** — refined targeting, new gap-closing animations, dynamic camera angles, Sign-based
finishers.

**Systems and feel** — improved traversal and movement, new witcher Sign effects, revamped mount
handling, enhanced monster behaviours, a **skill tree revamp**, **transmogrification**, and an
expanded photo mode.

The skill tree revamp and transmog are the two that reach furthest into how the game actually plays
rather than how it looks. We have broken the full list down separately.

## The timing

*Remastered* arrives twenty-six days after **The Blood of Dawnwalker**, the dark-fantasy RPG from
Rebel Wolves — a studio founded by *The Witcher 3*'s own director. Two sets of people who built this
game are shipping within a month of each other, one on the original and one on something new. That
is a coincidence of calendars rather than a fight, but it is a striking one, and we have written
about it separately.

CDPR ran guided presentations in **Hall 8.1** at gamescom and is streaming **REDstreams** sessions on
both *Remastered* and *Songs of the Past*.
