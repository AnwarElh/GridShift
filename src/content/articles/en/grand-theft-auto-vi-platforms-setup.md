Grand Theft Auto VI releases on **November 19, 2026**. Preparing for it is a shorter job than usual,
because Rockstar has announced far less about the technical side than a launch this close normally
comes with. Here is what is actually decided.

## The platform list, and the one that is missing

**PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X|S**. That is the complete list.

**No PC version has been announced.** Not dated, not delayed, not confirmed to be in development.
Rockstar has a well-known history of bringing its games to PC later, and that history is a reason to
expect something eventually — it is not an announcement, and Gridshift is not going to treat a
pattern as a plan. If you only own a PC, the honest position today is that this game does not have a
version for you.

There is no PS4 or Xbox One version either. Current-generation consoles only.

## The November 12 preload window

**Preloads and downloads begin November 12**, a week before the game unlocks.

That gap is the useful part of this whole page. A week is a lot of preload runway, and it exists
because the alternative — several million people pulling a very large file simultaneously on
November 19 — does not work. Treat it as the actual deadline rather than launch day: if you intend
to play on release, the thing to have done by November 12 is free the space and start the download,
not queue behind everyone who waited.

Rockstar has **not published an install size**. So the practical advice is unsatisfying but correct:
clear more room than you think you need, before the 12th, while you still have the option of
uninstalling something else calmly.

## What has not been announced, and why it matters to your setup

This is the part that would normally decide which console version to buy, and none of it exists yet.

- **No performance modes.** No frame rate targets, no resolution figures, no quality-versus-
  performance breakdown.
- **No Series X versus Series S detail.** Both are on the platform list; nothing has been said about
  how they differ.
- **No install size**, as above.
- **No review or preview embargo date**, which means there is currently no known point before launch
  at which independent technical analysis will appear.

If you own both consoles and are trying to decide where to install it, there is no announced
information that answers that question. Anyone giving you a confident answer today is guessing.

The realistic sequence is that digital foundries and technical write-ups will appear at or just after
launch, which is after the **November 20** cutoff for the Vintage Vice City Pack. That timing is not
an accident and it is worth being aware of when deciding whether to buy early.

## The two dates to put in a calendar

**November 12.** Preloads open. Have the storage free by then.

**November 19.** Release, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

There is a third, if you are buying: **November 20** is the cutoff for the Vintage Vice City Pack,
which is included with any pre-order or purchase made before it. It is the day after release, which
means you do not have to pre-order to get it — you have to buy before the day after launch.

## One thing worth doing now

If you play on Xbox and have not checked which of your consoles the family is actually using, or if
your PS5 has been running with 200 GB free for two years because it is fine, this is the month to
tidy that up. Not because anyone has told us how big the game is — nobody has — but because the
window for doing it without pressure closes on November 12, and every launch of this size produces
the same wave of people discovering their storage situation at midnight.
