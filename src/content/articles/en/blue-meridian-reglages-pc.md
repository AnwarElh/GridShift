Blue Meridian is poorly optimised in its PC preview build, but two settings do most of the work.

## Lower these first

Ambient occlusion on "medium" gives back twenty per cent of your frame rate for almost no visual
difference. Reflections on "low" give back ten more.

## What makes no difference

Per-object motion blur, and tessellation quality above "medium": no measurable difference at 1440p.
