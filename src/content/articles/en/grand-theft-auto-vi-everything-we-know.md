Grand Theft Auto VI ships on **November 19, 2026**, on **PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S**. That
sentence has been rewritten twice already, which is the main reason to write the rest of this
carefully.

## The date, and the two delays behind it

The game was originally targeted at **2025**. It moved to **May 26, 2026**. It then moved again to
**November 19, 2026**, where it currently stands.

**Preloads and downloads begin November 12** — a full week before launch, which for a game of this
size is less a courtesy than an infrastructure decision.

Rockstar has given no indication of a third move. It also gave no indication of the second one until
it happened, so the reasonable position on a locked date seventy-six days out is: it is the date, and
dates have moved before.

## What has actually been shown

**"An Extended Look at GTA 6"** premiered on **Netflix on August 27, 2026**. It runs roughly **26
minutes**, of which just over 20 minutes is gameplay, and it was captured **in-engine on PS5**.
Rockstar posted it to YouTube and the game's site afterwards.

It peaked at **3,970,738 concurrent viewers** across platforms, making it the **biggest gaming
stream of 2026**.

What it showed: protagonists **Jason and Lucia** across the state of **Leonida**. It opens with the
pair collecting a shipment tied to **Boobie Ike**; the job goes wrong into a shootout, which is how
the video demonstrates gunplay, cover, and the two protagonists working together. It also covered
robberies, car theft, police chases, **character switching**, NPC interactions, side activities and
everyday life in the world.

That is the only extended gameplay footage that exists. Everything else circulating is a trailer, a
still, or somebody's reconstruction.

## Editions and pricing, as confirmed

- **Ultimate Edition: $99.99.** It includes premium vehicles, weapons and apparel threaded through
  the story rather than dropped in a menu.
- **Vintage Vice City Pack:** included with any pre-order or purchase made **before November 20,
  2026**.
- **A free month of GTA+** for digital pre-orders.

The **standard edition price has not been confirmed**. Neither has anything about physical
distribution beyond the pre-order terms above.

## What Rockstar has not said

This is the part worth keeping straight, because a launch this large generates a lot of confident
writing about things nobody has announced.

- **No PC version has been announced.** Not delayed, not dated, not confirmed to exist. PS5 and Xbox
  Series X|S are the platforms.
- **No standard edition price.**
- **No review or preview embargo date.**
- **No map size, mission count, or any of the numbers that circulate as fact every week.** None of
  those have come from Rockstar.

## The other thing about November 19

It lands in an already-stacked autumn, and it lands with a week of preload in front of it. For
anyone budgeting time or money across the last six weeks of the year, the two dates that matter are
**November 12** — when the download starts and the disk space becomes your problem — and **November
20**, the cutoff after which the Vintage Vice City Pack stops being attached to a purchase.

Gridshift will update this article as Rockstar confirms things, and will keep saying "unconfirmed"
about the rest for as long as it stays unconfirmed.
