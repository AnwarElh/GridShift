If you last played in Inazuma or Sumeru and are wondering whether there is a natural place to come
back, this is one: version 6.0 opened Nod-Krai, and the 6.x cycle is built around it.

## Where Nod-Krai sits

Nod-Krai is an autonomous region within southern Snezhnaya, with Nashgorod as its capital. Its
design draws on Slavic folklore and northern and central European motifs — fjords, Icelandic
mythology. Functionally it is the introduction to the Cryo nation the game has been pointing at since
launch without letting you visit.

## Why now is a reasonable re-entry point

A new region means a new map to open from scratch, which is the part of Genshin that survives time
away best: chests, waypoints and world quests do not require you to remember a plot. The 6.x cycle
has continued into Lantern Rite and further Nod-Krai map expansion, so there is a substantial block
of content sitting there rather than a single patch.

## What has not changed

The version rhythm is still roughly six weeks. Characters still arrive on limited banners. If you
left because of the gacha, nothing here will change your mind — Nod-Krai is a reason to return to
the exploration, not a structural reform.

## One practical note

Do not sprint the archon quest to catch up. Nod-Krai's map is the content; the story is the thread
that unlocks it. Players who rush the quest line and then look at a fully-marked map tend to bounce.
