*Final Fantasy VII Revelation* has a release date: **April 8, 2027**. Square Enix confirmed it
during the PlayStation State of Play on September 3, alongside nearly ten minutes of new
gameplay, as reported by **GameSpot**.

The trailer that carried the date put all eight playable party members on screen — Cloud, Tifa,
Barret, Red XIII, Yuffie, Cait Sith, Vincent and Cid — and leaned on team-up abilities: Vincent's
Galian Beast form charging a group alongside Red XIII, Tifa and Yuffie coming down together in a
dive kick and shuriken combination.

## The world map is the headline, not the date

Director **Naoki Hamaguchi** introduced a longer stretch of unedited gameplay, and it was spent
almost entirely on traversal. The Highwind flies over Gaia. You point it where you like. When you
reach somewhere worth landing, you pick a party from any of the eight characters and parachute
straight down.

That is a deliberate callback to the 1997 game's overworld, and it is the part of *Revelation*
that the two previous instalments could not offer. *Remake* was a corridor through one city;
*Rebirth* opened up but still handed you a curated map. A flyable world with instant party
selection at the point of landing is the trilogy finally reaching for the shape of the thing it
is remaking.

The demo then dropped into a Gongaga side quest featuring **Sotetsu**, a character from a
*Rebirth* side quest. Yuffie already knows him, and has a special interaction as a result — which
implies missions can play differently depending on who you bring. Square Enix did not say how
widely that applies.

## Two expansions, already listed

The Steam page moved faster than the announcement. **GameSpot** reports that two downloadable
story expansions appeared on the storefront: one called **"Sephiroth"**, the other **"Vincent and
the Turks"**. Both are listed for release between **Winter 2027 and April 2028**, with no further
detail attached.

Square Enix has since confirmed the Deluxe and Collector's Editions include both packs — so the
expansions are real, and the only thing the Steam listing gave away early was their names.

## And a Trilogy Edition

Hamaguchi also announced a **Trilogy Edition** collecting all three parts of the *Final Fantasy
VII Remake* trilogy in one package. No date, no price, no word on whether it is physical, digital
or both.

It is the obvious release to make and the obvious one to be cautious about: a trilogy bundle
announced before the third game ships tends to arrive after it, and often long after. Treat it as
confirmed to exist and nothing more.
