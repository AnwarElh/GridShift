**Final Fantasy VII Revelation** releases on **April 8, 2027**, on **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S,
Nintendo Switch 2 and Windows**. Sony's **State of Play on September 3** carried the date, a new
trailer and fresh gameplay, and pre-orders opened the same evening.

That is the third and final part of the remake trilogy, and the first time in fifteen months that
anybody outside Square Enix has been able to write a specific day next to it.

## Gamescom set this up badly, and the correction came fast

*Revelation* was announced at **Summer Game Fest in June 2026**. Its next appearance was a short
segment at **Gamescom Opening Night Live** in late August, which drew a genuinely poor reaction —
not for what it showed, but for how little of it there was and for the absence of the one thing the
audience had turned up for.

Two weeks later the date arrived on somebody else's stage. Read that sequence however you like; the
practical outcome is that the finale of a decade-long project got its release day at a Sony showcase
rather than at the event where the tease had already spent the goodwill.

## Four platforms, three PC storefronts, and Play Anywhere

The platform list is the widest the trilogy has had.

- **PS5**, **Xbox Series X|S**, **Nintendo Switch 2** and **Windows**, all on April 8, 2027.
- The Windows version launches on **Steam, the Epic Games Store and the Microsoft Store
  simultaneously** — a first for this series, which has previously staggered or skipped storefronts.
- The Microsoft Store version supports **Play Anywhere cross-progression** with Xbox Series X|S.

This is the endpoint of a move that has been running all year. *Remake Intergrade* and *Rebirth*,
both once PS5-and-Windows games, reached **Switch 2 and Xbox Series X|S during 2026**, with *Rebirth*
arriving on Switch 2 on **June 3**. The finale does not have to catch up, because the catching up
already happened.

## The Switch 2 physical release is a Game-Key Card

Worth stating plainly, because it is the kind of detail that gets discovered at the till: the
**Switch 2 physical edition is a Game-Key Card**, listed at **130 GB**.

A Game-Key Card is a cartridge that authorises a download rather than holding the game. If you are
buying physical on Switch 2, you are buying a licence in a box and 130 GB of storage is your problem.
That is not a scandal — it is increasingly normal for games this size — but it is a purchase decision
and the box art will not make it for you.

## Editions: three games cost less than one deluxe

Pre-orders went live with four digital tiers:

- **Trilogy Edition — $84.99.** *Remake Intergrade*, *Rebirth* and *Revelation*, plus the pre-order
  bonus. Square Enix says this price stands until **April 7**, the day before release.
- **Standard — $69.99.** *Revelation* and the pre-order bonus.
- **Premium — $99.99.** Adds the **Story Expansion Pass** and **24-hour early access**.
- **Premium Plus — $109.99.** The above with further content.

A **Collector's Edition** was also revealed.

The arithmetic there is the interesting part and it is not a typo: the bundle containing **three
games** costs **$15 less** than the deluxe edition containing one. Anyone who has already bought
*Remake* and *Rebirth* is, in effect, being charged more than a newcomer for less software. We have
written that ladder up separately.

The **Story Expansion Pass** covers two post-launch story add-ons — **the first centred on
Sephiroth, the second on Vincent and the Turks** — tentatively scheduled for **Winter 2027 to Spring
2028**.

## What is in the game, as confirmed

*Revelation* picks up directly from *Rebirth* and adapts the remainder of the 1997 original, from the
party's arrival at the **Great Glacier** through to the confrontation with Sephiroth at the
**Northern Crater**. **Wutai** and **Rocket Town**, both absent from *Rebirth*, feature prominently.
**Underwater traversal by submarine** is confirmed, along with a new Chocobo mechanic unrelated to
the original's breeding system.

Combat is built on a new system called **FITS**, designed by combat director **Teruki Endo**, which
lets each character shift play style according to *Final Fantasy* **jobs**. Director **Naoki
Hamaguchi** has framed it as an extension of *Rebirth*'s combat rather than a replacement.

On minigames — a running argument since *Rebirth* — *Queen's Blood* returns in an enhanced form, and
material from later in the original, including the **Great Glacier snowboarding**, has been
reimagined to sit closer to the narrative.

The game runs on a **modified Unreal Engine 4**, retained deliberately after internal debate.

## Three years, and why

*Revelation* has had the **shortest production cycle of the three games**. Reporting by **Jason
Schreier for Bloomberg (June 12, 2026)** attributes that to Square Enix **retaining the vast majority
of the core team** across *Remake*, *Rebirth* and now the finale, rather than disbanding staff
between projects as it has elsewhere.

## The Sephiroth voice change

**Travis Willingham replaces Tyler Hoechlin as the English voice of Sephiroth.** Square Enix has said
Hoechlin was unavailable during *Revelation*'s production. The Japanese performance is unchanged:
**Toshiyuki Morikawa** returns.

## What has not been said

- **No PC system requirements**, and no performance targets on any platform.
- **No file sizes** beyond the 130 GB attached to the Switch 2 Game-Key Card listing.
- **No review or preview embargo.** Nobody outside Square Enix has played this.
