Scopely's update 94.1 is not the kind that leads with a new ship. It reworks the seven-day login
streak, updates Alliance Tournaments, improves reward claiming, adds bulk research and touches Wave
Defense — and, notably, introduces new measures against chat spam.

## Server transfers were the real complaint

The update also ships improved server transfers, described as faster and more reliable, explicitly
addressing recent transfer problems. For a game whose social structure is built on alliances,
transfers failing is not a cosmetic issue: it strands players away from the people they play with.

## The preceding update

94, *Cause & Effect*, introduced Choice Tokens in the Battle Pass, usable across three Exchanges to
claim artifacts and ship blueprints. Together the pair reads as a maintenance cycle — an established
game tightening its systems rather than expanding them.

## Where this sits

Scopely has published a 2026 roadmap preview outlining the themes guiding the year. Eight years after
launch, an update that spends its budget on bulk research and chat moderation is a reasonable
indicator of where a live game's actual friction is — and those are usually the changes long-term
players notice most.
