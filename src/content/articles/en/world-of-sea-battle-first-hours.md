World of Sea Battle has been in early access since October 2025, and it is an open-world naval MMO
rather than a match-based one — the map is shared, and what you do on it persists. That changes what
a first session should look like.

## Take a combat ship, not the fast one

Four broad classes matter early. Combat ships balance speed and firepower and are the sane beginner
choice. High-speed ships are fast and fragile, useful for solo scouting and not much else yet.
Transports carry enormous cargo and will lose almost every fight they enter. Heavy hulls are slow and
absorb punishment, which matters in arena and guild war rather than on day one. Imperial ships have
the best stats in the game and require rare currencies to obtain.

## Trading pays better than fighting, early

The most reliable early gold comes from the supply-and-demand spread between ports: buy where a good
is abundant, sail to where it is wanted. The north-to-south sugar and salt run is the standard
starting circuit for a reason — it is short, it is safe enough, and it funds your first real ship.

## Capture small pirates instead of sinking them

Small pirate vessels can be captured and added to your fleet rather than destroyed. Early on, an
extra hull is worth more than the loot from sinking one.

## The mistake that actually costs you

Battle Marks, Voodoo Skulls and Escudos are the rare currencies behind Imperial ships and endgame
content, and they cannot be repurchased. Selling them for early gold is the single most common
beginner error in this game. The gold is replaceable. They are not.
