**The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Remastered** will be sold on **Battle.net** when it launches on
**29 September 2026**, through a new partnership between **CD PROJEKT RED** and **Blizzard
Entertainment**. The studio describes it as "a collaboration three decades in the making".

It is a small line in a large announcement, and it is the most unusual part of it.

## What the deal actually contains

Two things have been confirmed, and they are worth separating.

**The storefront.** *Remastered* and the forthcoming expansion **Songs of the Past** will both be
available on Battle.net. That makes five PC storefronts for this game — Steam, GOG, the Epic Games
Store, Xbox on PC and now Blizzard's — for a title that has historically been most associated with
GOG, CD PROJEKT's own platform.

**The cosmetic.** Buying *Songs of the Past* on Battle.net unlocks a **Geralt-inspired skin in
Diablo IV**. Further details are due at **BlizzCon in September**.

That is the whole of it. No other Blizzard title has been mentioned, no Witcher content is going the
other way, and nothing has been said about Battle.net exclusivity for anything.

## Why it is strange

Battle.net is not a general storefront. For most of its existence it has sold Blizzard's own games
and essentially nothing else. A third-party single-player RPG appearing there is a departure from
how the platform has been positioned for nearly thirty years — which is presumably what CD PROJEKT
RED means by the phrase about three decades.

The Diablo IV skin is the more conventional half. Cross-promotional cosmetics tied to a purchase on
a specific storefront are a standard mechanism for moving buyers onto a platform, and Geralt is a
recognisable enough silhouette to work as one.

## What it does not change

Worth stating plainly, because a new storefront invites assumptions:

- **The free upgrade is unaffected.** Existing owners on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Steam, GOG and the
  Epic Games Store still get *Remastered* at no cost. Battle.net is a place to buy the game, not an
  upgrade path.
- **Nothing suggests Battle.net exclusivity**, for the base game or the expansion.
- **Songs of the Past is still only dated "2027"**, on Battle.net as everywhere else.

## The rest of the announcement

The Battle.net deal arrived alongside the *Remastered* date, the reveal of *Songs of the Past* —
a paid 2027 expansion set in the new region of **Letten**, co-developed with **Fool's Theory** — and
a giveaway that took effect immediately: **every current owner of The Witcher 3 now has Hearts of
Stone and Blood and Wine for free**.

*Remastered* itself is co-developed with **Yigsoft** and launches on PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S and,
for the first time natively, **Nintendo Switch 2**.
