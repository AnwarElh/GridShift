Ninefold spent a year repairing Iron District, and the result deserves a second look. Version 2.1
rewrites the cover system, the part of the game everyone held against it.

## What changed

Cover used to be probabilistic and unreadable; it is now deterministic and displayed. You know what
you are risking before you confirm a move. That is the difference between a tactics game and a dice
game.

## What remains

The campaign runs out of steam in its final third: the same three objectives, in the same three
settings. And still no co-op, despite the announcement of a four-player mode for winter.
