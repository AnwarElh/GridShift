Enlisted is free on every platform it ships on, which makes "where should I install it" a question
about convenience rather than cost. The answer is less obvious than it looks, because the platform
list and the cross-play list are the same list — and that is unusual.

## The platforms

PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S, and PC. Cross-play covers all of them, plus
the Gaijin.Net service on PC alongside Steam. Steam Deck is supported.

## What full cross-play changes

Because cross-play spans every platform including both previous-generation consoles, the matchmaking
pool is not fragmented by hardware. For a squad-based shooter this matters more than it does for a
single-player-facing game: queue times and lobby quality depend on population, and a game that splits
its population across five platforms without cross-play degrades quickly on the smaller ones.

It also means your platform choice is not a commitment to a community. You are not picking which
players you will meet.

## The PC detail

On PC there are two routes — Steam and Gaijin.Net — and they play together. If you already run other
Gaijin titles, the launcher is shared; if you would rather keep everything in one library, Steam is
the straightforward option. Neither is disadvantaged in matchmaking.

## Practical recommendation

If you own a current-generation console and a modest PC, install it on the console: the game is
built for controller play and the cross-play removes the usual reason to prefer PC. If you want the
Steam Deck, it is supported and the game's squad interface survives the smaller screen better than
most shooters.
