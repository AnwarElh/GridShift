*Final Fantasy VII Revelation* arrives **April 8, 2027**, and Square Enix has detailed the full
edition lineup. The details below come from Square Enix's own breakdown as published by
**GameSpot** on September 3. Prices are US dollars; regional pricing was not announced.

## Physical — PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2

**Standard — $69.99.** The game. Day 1 Editions at several retailers add a *Magic: The
Gathering — FINAL FANTASY* Final Showdown promo card. Amazon's Day 1 Edition adds a folded
poster instead.

**Deluxe — $99.99.** Adds an artbook and a steelbook case, plus a *Magic: The Gathering — FINAL
FANTASY* promotional card with Day 1 Editions at participating retailers.

**Collector's Edition — $349.99, Square Enix Store only.** Everything in the Deluxe, plus the
collector's contents, and it carries the two story expansions along with the in-game
**Reclaimant Choker** accessory and a Survival Set of consumables.

## Digital — PS5, PC, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2

**Standard — $69.99.** The game.

**Premium — $99.99.** Adds the two story expansions, the Reclaimant Choker and the Survival Set.

**Premium Plus — $109.99.** Everything in Premium, plus a digital mini soundtrack and a digital
artbook.

Every digital edition carries the same pre-order incentive: the **Summon Materia: Mecha-Chocobo
& Moogle**.

## The one thing to actually decide

The two story expansions — **"Sephiroth"** and **"Vincent and the Turks"**, both listed on Steam
for release between Winter 2027 and April 2028 — are the only content that changes what you play
rather than what sits on your shelf.

They are included in exactly three editions: physical Collector's ($349.99), digital Premium
($99.99), and digital Premium Plus ($109.99).

That is the trap in the lineup. Physical Deluxe and digital Premium are both $99.99, and they are
not equivalent. The physical one gets you an artbook and a steelbook; the digital one gets you
the expansions. If you buy the $99.99 box expecting the DLC because the digital tier at the same
price includes it, you will be buying the expansions again later.

## What we would do

If you want the expansions and do not care about objects, **digital Premium at $99.99** is the
whole decision — Premium Plus adds $10 for a soundtrack and an artbook you view on a screen.

If you want a disc, buy the **physical Standard at $69.99** and add the expansions digitally when
they are actually out in 2028, rather than paying $30 now for an artbook and a steelbook. Note
that Square Enix has not announced standalone pricing for the expansions, so this is a judgement
call, not arithmetic.

And a physical caveat worth knowing before you pre-order a box: **Push Square** reported on
September 3 that the PS5 release ships on a single disc with a required digital download. A
physical copy of *Revelation* is not a copy you can play without downloading.
