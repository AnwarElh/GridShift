Northlight shipped patch 4.2a on Wednesday morning. It doubles component income in district one,
raises the useful stamina threshold from 40 to 45, and cuts the cost of the first workbench
upgrades.

It is the first time the studio has revisited first-act balance since release, after three months
of consistent feedback. We replayed our review and raised its score accordingly.
