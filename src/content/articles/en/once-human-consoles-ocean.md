NetEase and Starry Studio brought Once Human to Xbox Series S|X and PlayStation 5 during Opening
Night Live at Gamescom 2026, two years after the PC release and roughly sixteen months after mobile.

The console version is not a straight port. It adds a first-person perspective players can switch to
and from freely — a meaningful change for a survival game built third-person — alongside full
cross-play and cross-progression with the existing platforms. PlayStation 5 gets DualSense-specific
features; on Xbox the game arrived day one on Game Pass with launch perks attached.

## The bigger news is October

Announced alongside the console launch: **Isles of Abyss**, an ocean expansion the studio is calling
its largest content update yet, scheduled for October. It brings ship building and combat on the high
seas — a direction the game has not previously gone.

That framing is worth noting. A survival game adding naval construction and sea combat is not a
seasonal event; it is a second axis of play. Whether it lands as a genuine expansion of the loop or a
mode bolted to the side is the thing to watch in October.
