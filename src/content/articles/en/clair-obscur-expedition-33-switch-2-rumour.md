Every few weeks, a fresh round of "Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 is coming to Switch 2" posts goes
around. The underlying material has not changed since June 2026, and it is thinner than the
headlines make it look.

## What was actually said

In June 2026, the leaker known as **Nate the Hate** reaffirmed that Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 is
coming to Switch 2 "this year". Nate the Hate is not a random account — he is credited with the
Ocarina of Time and Star Fox remake calls, which is why the claim travels as far as it does.

Separately, and earlier, Sandfall Interactive CEO **Guillaume Broche** had discussed the possibility
of a Switch 2 version. Discussed the possibility. Not announced it, not dated it, not committed to it.

That is the complete evidence base. Everything else circulating is one of those two things
paraphrased.

## What has not happened

There is no announcement from Sandfall Interactive. There is no announcement from Kepler
Interactive. There is no Nintendo eShop listing, no Nintendo Direct segment, no release window, no
price, and no confirmation that a port is in development at all rather than under consideration.

A leaker's track record is a reason to take a claim seriously. It is not a substitute for the thing
being confirmed, and "this year" from a third party in June is not a date. It is a nine-month window
that, as of early September, has four months left in it.

## Why the claim is plausible anyway

The commercial logic is not hard to follow. The game has sold eight million copies as of April 2026
and won Game of the Year at The Game Awards, DICE, the GDC Awards and BAFTA. It already runs on PS5,
PC and Xbox Series X/S, and it was on Game Pass day one. A turn-based RPG is also, historically, the
kind of game that does disproportionately well on a Nintendo handheld.

Sandfall has also kept shipping: the free Thank You Update added a location, extra late-game bosses,
costumes and languages, and a June 30, 2026 patch brought Arabic in, taking supported languages to
twenty. A studio doing that much post-launch work is not a studio that has moved on.

Plausible is not the same as announced. Both things are true at once, and the honest position is to
hold them together rather than picking whichever one makes a better headline.

## What would change this

An eShop listing. A Nintendo Direct or Partner Showcase slot. A dated press release from Sandfall or
Kepler. Any of those would move this from rumour to news, and Gridshift will update the article
when one arrives.

Until then: if you are holding off on buying Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 because you want to play it
portable, you are waiting on an unconfirmed port with no date. That may be a fine bet. It should be
made knowing it is a bet.
