Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 released on April 24, 2025, and the platform question has not changed
since — which is exactly why it keeps being asked. Here is the current state of it, with the part
everyone hopes has changed answered first.

## The platform list, complete

**PlayStation 5. Windows PC. Xbox Series X/S.** That is the list. It was the list at launch and it
is the list today. The Steam appid is 1903340.

There is no PS4 version, no Xbox One version, and no handheld version of any kind. Nothing has been
added to that list in sixteen months.

## Game Pass is still the cheapest way in

The game was available on **Xbox Game Pass on day one**, and it has stayed there. If you already
subscribe, the decision is made for you: install it on Xbox Series or on PC through the Game Pass
app and start.

This is worth spelling out because the game's reputation now suggests a full-price prestige
purchase. It has been a subscription title since the day it shipped, which makes trying it a much
smaller commitment than the awards shelf implies. If you bounce off a turn-based RPG in the first
few hours, that is a subscription month, not sixty euros.

## Twenty languages, and when Arabic arrived

Language support expanded through the free **Thank You Update**, and a patch on **June 30, 2026**
added Arabic, bringing the total to **twenty supported languages**.

Two practical notes. First, if your copy has not been patched since before June 2026 — an offline
install, a console that has been off for a year — the language you want may exist in the game but
not in your build. Update before concluding it is not there.

Second, the honest framing: twenty languages is a strong number for a studio this size, and Arabic
arriving fourteen months after launch, on a game that had sold eight million copies by then, is late.
Both are true, and if that language is the one you need, the good news is that it is now in.

## Switch 2 is not a platform for this game

This is the question in every thread, so it gets a plain answer: **there is no Switch 2 version, and
none has been announced.**

What exists is a claim. The leaker Nate the Hate — a credible one, credited with the Ocarina of Time
and Star Fox remake calls — reaffirmed in June 2026 that the game lands on Switch 2 "this year".
Sandfall CEO Guillaume Broche had previously discussed the possibility. Neither is an announcement,
a date, or a store listing.

So: do not plan a purchase around it, do not delay a purchase because of it, and treat any "coming
to Switch 2" headline you see as a restatement of that same June claim until a first-party listing
says otherwise.

## Picking between the three that exist

If you have Game Pass, use it, on whichever of Xbox Series or PC you already play on. There is no
version of the "which platform" question that beats "the one you are already paying for".

If you are buying, the choice is mostly about your existing hardware rather than the game. PS5 and
Xbox Series X/S are the fixed, no-configuration options; PC is where you have control over
resolution and frame rate and where the Steam version lives. A turn-based RPG is one of the least
input-sensitive genres there is, which means a controller on a sofa is a perfectly good default and
there is no competitive reason to prefer mouse and keyboard.

If what you actually want is to play it in bed on a handheld, the honest answer is that the game
does not currently support that in any officially announced form, and Gridshift is not going to
pretend otherwise until someone with the authority to announce it does.

## Where this page stops

We are not listing per-option accessibility settings here, because we are not going to describe menu
contents we cannot verify against a current build for every platform. What we can state is the part
that materially changes who can play it: the language coverage above, and the fact that the hardest
content in the game — the Endless Tower added in the Thank You Update — is optional and gated behind
nothing.
