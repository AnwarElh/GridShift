The extended *Grand Theft Auto VI* gameplay trailer that premiered on Netflix last month is now
running on Rockstar's official Bilibili channel in a noticeably different state. Suggestive
dancing, underwear, smoking and bloody bodies are blurred out. In one shot of Jason crossing a
nightclub, so much of the frame is covered that there is barely a shot left.

The edits were first documented by a *GTA Forums* user posting as BDF, spotted by BenVideoTech,
and **Kotaku** says it confirmed them independently on September 4. The censored version was
still live on Bilibili at that point.

## The detail that makes this odd

Rockstar did not upload a censored cut and leave it at that. It uploaded the **unedited**
extended trailer to Bilibili first. That version stayed up until roughly **August 31**, when it
was reportedly swapped for the blurred one.

Two things sit awkwardly next to that. Rockstar's earlier *GTA 6* trailers are still on the same
Bilibili channel with no censorship at all — it is only the extended look that got treated this
way. And, per Kotaku, you can open Rockstar's own website inside China, without a VPN, and watch
the uncut gameplay video with Chinese subtitles.

So the same company is serving the clean version and the blurred version into the same market at
the same time, and only one of the two is on the platform where most people will actually see it.

## What is claimed, and by whom

This is worth keeping straight, because the reporting and the speculation are different things.

What is documented: the blurring, the swap date, the fact that older trailers are untouched. What
is claimed by BDF and not independently confirmed: that Take-Two has also been chasing down
re-uploads of the uncensored trailer, with a screenshot offered from a friend who says their
upload was pulled.

What is opinion: BDF reports that Chinese players are split on the reading — some take it as
Rockstar keeping adult content away from minors, others as Take-Two assuming the market is, in
their words, closed-off and backward, and pre-emptively cutting more than anyone asked it to.

Kotaku says it approached Rockstar for comment. There has been no answer.

## Why it matters beyond one video

*GTA 6* has not shipped, and every asset Rockstar releases is doing double duty as marketing and
as a statement about what the game is. A self-applied edit is not a regulator's decision — no
Chinese authority has been shown to have ordered this — which makes it a choice about how the
game presents itself in a market Take-Two clearly wants.

Nothing here says the game itself will ship differently in China. It says the trailer did, on one
platform, nine days ago, and that the company has not said why.
