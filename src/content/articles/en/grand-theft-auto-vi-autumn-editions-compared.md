Three of the games this site covers have edition ladders open right now, and they are structured
very differently. One rewards newcomers over returning players. One asks a hundred dollars. One puts
its extras in a box. Here is what each actually contains.

## The one that costs $99.99

**Grand Theft Auto VI** ships 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preloads from 12 November.

| Tier | Price | Contains |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | **Not announced** | — |
| **Ultimate Edition** | **$99.99** | Premium vehicles, weapons and apparel threaded through Jason and Lucia's story |

Two things are worth stating plainly.

**The standard edition price has not been announced.** At the time of writing, the only confirmed
number is $99.99 for the top tier. Anyone quoting a base price is guessing.

**Every purchase before 20 November includes the Vintage Vice City Pack**, and digital pre-orders
also get a free month of GTA+. That is a pre-order incentive rather than an edition difference — it
applies whichever tier you buy.

What the Ultimate Edition contains is in-game cosmetic and vehicle content. It does not include
extra story, and no expansion pass has been announced.

## The one where three games cost less than one

**Final Fantasy VII Revelation** ships 8 April 2027. Its ladder has the oddest shape on this page.

| Tier | Price | Contains |
|---|---|---|
| **Trilogy Edition** | **$84.99** | *Remake Intergrade* + *Rebirth* + *Revelation* + pre-order bonus |
| Standard | $69.99 | *Revelation* + pre-order bonus |
| Premium | $99.99 | *Revelation* + Story Expansion Pass + 24-hour early access |
| Premium Plus | $109.99 | The above, plus further content |

Read the first two rows together. **The bundle containing three games costs $15 less than the deluxe
edition containing one.** Square Enix says the Trilogy Edition price stands until 7 April, the day
before release.

The consequence is unavoidable: someone who already owns *Remake* and *Rebirth* is being asked for
more money, for less software, than someone arriving fresh. Loyalty is priced worse than novelty
here.

The **Story Expansion Pass**, included from Premium up, covers two post-launch story add-ons — the
first centred on Sephiroth, the second on Vincent and the Turks — tentatively scheduled for winter
2027 to spring 2028.

One further detail for physical buyers: **the Switch 2 physical release is a Game-Key Card**, listed
at 130 GB. A cartridge that authorises a download rather than carrying the game.

## The one with a physical collector's box

**The Blood of Dawnwalker** launched 3 September on PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC.

| Tier | Contains |
|---|---|
| Standard | The game |
| **Eclipse Edition** | Adds the soundtrack and a digital comic |
| **Collector's Edition** | The Eclipse contents physically, plus a world map and a figure |

Rebel Wolves has not published headline prices in the same form as the other two, so this table
covers contents rather than cost.

A quirk worth knowing: **there are two PC builds** for different age ratings by region. Rebel Wolves
has not stated that the two are otherwise equivalent, and we have not compared them.

## The pattern: what a deluxe tier actually contains

Across all three, the extras fall into four kinds — and only one of them is more game:

1. **Cosmetics and vehicles** (GTA VI Ultimate). No additional story.
2. **Physical objects** (Dawnwalker Collector's). A map, a figure, a soundtrack.
3. **Early access** (FF7 Premium). Twenty-four hours.
4. **Actual future content** (FF7 Story Expansion Pass). Two story add-ons, dated tentatively.

Only the fourth is more game, and only one of the three ladders offers it. That is the useful lens:
a deluxe tier that adds cosmetics is a different purchase from one that adds chapters, even when
both cost $99.99.

## What we would buy

**GTA VI:** wait for the standard price before deciding. The Ultimate Edition's contents are
cosmetic, and the pre-order bonus applies at any tier before 20 November — so there is no deadline
pressure to buy up.

**FF7 Revelation:** if you own neither earlier game, the Trilogy Edition at $84.99 is the clearest
value on this page by a distance. If you own both already, Standard at $69.99 is the honest pick and
the Premium tier is only worth it if you want the Story Expansion Pass at full price, sight unseen,
for content dated no more precisely than "winter 2027 to spring 2028".

**Dawnwalker:** the Eclipse contents are a soundtrack and a comic. Buy the standard edition unless
you specifically want the physical objects in the Collector's.

We have covered GTA VI's and FF7's edition ladders individually in more depth.
