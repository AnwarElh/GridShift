FunPlus has begun rolling Season 2 into Foundation: Galactic Frontier, with servers 1001 through
1016 scheduled first during August.

A staged rollout by server block is standard for the genre, and it tells you something about how the
game is run: seasons here are server-scoped events rather than a single global flip, so which server
you are on determines when your world changes.

## The game's first year

Galactic Frontier launched globally on 21 October 2025 across iOS, Android and PC, built by FunPlus's
Chengdu studio in collaboration with Paramount Television Studios and drawing on the Foundation
licence. A Steam version followed in January 2026, free to play.

## The licence question

Licensed strategy MMOs live or die on whether the fiction does any work beyond the cover art. A
Foundation game has more to draw on than most — Asimov's premise is about predicting and steering the
long arc of a civilisation, which is closer to what a 4X actually simulates than most licences
manage. Season 2's rollout is the first real test of whether the game is using that.
