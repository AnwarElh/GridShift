A lot of people bought Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 during the awards run, played it, finished it,
and put it down before the free update existed. If that is you, the game on your drive today is not
the game you played, and the difference is worth understanding before you reinstall.

## What the update actually contains

The **Thank You Update** was released by Sandfall Interactive to mark five million copies sold,
after the Game of the Year win. It is free, and it adds:

- a new location
- extra late-game bosses
- new costumes
- expanded language support
- an **Endless Tower** of revamped boss fights

A later patch on **June 30, 2026** added Arabic, bringing supported languages to **twenty**.

That list is short and it is not padding. New location, more bosses, and a dedicated boss-rush
structure is a content drop aimed at the end of the game, not the middle of it.

## It costs nothing, and there is nothing to buy

There is no season pass to look for and no paid expansion sitting next to it. The update is a patch:
if the game is installed and updated, you have it. If you bought through Game Pass — the game was
available there day one — the same applies.

This is worth stating plainly because the reflex, in 2026, is to assume that "new content for the
Game of the Year" means a storefront page. It does not here.

## Finish the campaign first — this is not a re-entry point

The single most common mistake with content like this is treating it as a way back in. It is not.
Everything the update adds sits at or past the end of the campaign: late-game bosses are late-game
bosses, and a boss-rush tower assumes you have already learned what the campaign taught you.

If you never finished Clair Obscur: Expedition 33, the correct move is to ignore the update entirely
until you have. If you finished it a year ago and remember the shape of it but not the detail, budget
some time to get your hands back before you go looking for the new fights. Returning to a
turn-based RPG after twelve months and walking straight into its hardest content is how a
reinstall becomes a refund.

## The Endless Tower is the difficulty spike

Kotaku's headline on the update was that Clair Obscur's free DLC is "curb stomping even its best
players". That is not outlet hyperbole; it is the design brief.

The Tower is built for the fraction of the player base that exhausted the campaign's difficulty and
wanted more, and it does not ease anyone in. It is not scaled to "you finished the game once". It is
scaled to people who kept playing after that.

The practical consequence: expect to lose, and expect to lose early. A first failed run in the Tower
is not a signal that you have forgotten how to play or that your build is wrong. It is the intended
first result. Sandfall did not build a difficulty ramp here; it built a wall and left a door in it
for the people who can get through.

## What to do if the Tower stops you

Three honest options, in the order most people should try them.

1. **Go and do the rest of the update first.** The new location and the extra late-game bosses are
   the part of the drop that behaves like normal content. They are the sane place to spend your
   returning hours, and they are where most players will get their money's worth out of an update
   that cost nothing.
2. **Stop and treat the Tower as optional.** It is optional. Nothing in the campaign, and nothing in
   the rest of the update, is gated behind clearing it. Deciding that a boss-rush mode built for the
   top of the skill curve is not for you is a legitimate outcome, not a failure state.
3. **Come back to it as its own project.** If you do want it, treat it as a separate thing you are
   playing rather than a coda to the campaign. That reframing is most of the battle: people bounce
   off the Tower hardest when they expected it to be an epilogue.

## The thing the game explains badly

There is very little in the presentation that tells a returning player which of the new content is
"more of the game" and which is "the hard mode for veterans". They arrived in the same update and
they are described in the same breath, and they are not remotely the same experience.

If you take one thing from this guide: the new location and the extra bosses are for you. The
Endless Tower is for a specific, smaller audience, and the free update is still worth installing
even if you never open its door.
