*The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Remastered* arrives on **September 29, 2026**. For a large majority of
the people reading this, it is free and already accounted for. The useful work is figuring out which
category you are in, because two of the platforms it launches on are new purchases rather than
upgrades.

## Which platforms get it free

*Remastered* is a **free upgrade** for existing owners on:

- **PlayStation 5**
- **Xbox Series X|S**
- **Steam**
- **GOG**
- **Epic Games Store**

If your copy of *The Witcher 3* lives on any of those, you are done. Nothing to buy, nothing to
claim in advance as far as CDPR has described it.

Worth noting for anyone whose copy predates the current generation: the game's 2022 next-gen update
was itself a free upgrade from the PS4 and Xbox One versions, so an old console purchase has, in most
cases, already carried forward once. If you own it on **PS4 or Xbox One**, CDPR's announcement does
not specifically address you — its wording covers PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. That is a gap rather than
a refusal, and we would not assume either way until the studio is clearer.

## Check your library now: you already own the expansions

This is the part worth acting on today, and it costs nothing.

**Every current owner of The Witcher 3 has been given Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine for free**,
effective from the gamescom announcement — not on September 29, but already.

Those were paid expansions for eleven years. *Blood and Wine* in particular is a thirty-plus hour
campaign in a region, Toussaint, that a lot of people have never seen because it sat behind a
separate purchase. If you bought the base game years ago and never went back, it is in your library
now, and it is arguably a bigger deal than the remaster itself.

Go and look. Then, if you intend to replay in remastered form, consider *not* starting it until the
29th.

## The two platforms that are not free upgrades

*Remastered* also launches on two platforms where no prior ownership exists to upgrade from:

- **Nintendo Switch 2.** This is the first **native** version of *The Witcher 3* on a Nintendo
  console. The 2019 original-Switch release was a cloud-assisted port with substantial compromises,
  and CDPR has not said whether owning that version entitles you to anything here. Treat the
  Switch 2 version as a new purchase unless CDPR says otherwise.
- **Battle.net.** New storefront, via the CD PROJEKT RED and Blizzard partnership. Buying the
  forthcoming *Songs of the Past* expansion on Battle.net unlocks a **Geralt-inspired skin in
  Diablo IV**; details are due at **BlizzCon in September**.

Neither is an upgrade path. Both are places to buy the game if you do not have it.

## What to do before the 29th

A short list, all of it free:

1. **Confirm which storefront your copy is on.** That single fact decides everything else.
2. **Claim or verify Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine.** They should simply be there; check that
   they are.
3. **Do not delete your save files.** CDPR has not made a statement about save compatibility between
   the current version and *Remastered*, and there is a **skill tree revamp** in the update — which
   is exactly the kind of change that can complicate an in-progress character. Keep the saves and
   wait for the detail.
4. **Do not start a fresh playthrough this month** if you can wait twenty-five days. The visual and
   combat work is substantial enough that a first run is better spent on the new version.
5. **If you are buying for Switch 2**, wait for performance information. There is none yet, and a
   native version of a 2015 open-world game on a handheld is exactly the case where it matters.

## The questions CDPR has not answered

Stated plainly, because the gaps are real and the launch is close:

- **Save compatibility** between the current build and *Remastered*.
- **Whether PS4 and Xbox One owners qualify** for the free upgrade.
- **Whether the 2019 Switch version confers anything** toward the Switch 2 release.
- **Performance targets on any platform**, Switch 2 included.
- **PC system requirements** for the path-traced configuration.
- **Whether the upgrade is automatic** or has to be claimed on each storefront.

We will update this guide as CD PROJEKT RED answers them. Until then, the reliable facts are the two
at the top: five storefronts get it free, and the expansions are already yours.
