Version 6.0 opened Nod-Krai, the autonomous region of southern Snezhnaya with Nashgorod as its
capital. What has happened since is worth noting on its own: rather than closing the region and
pointing at the next one, the 6.x versions have kept enlarging it, with further map expansion
arriving alongside the cycle's seasonal content.

## A change of shape, not just of pace

Genshin's established rhythm was one major region per arc, opened more or less whole, then a run of
story and event patches on top of it. Nod-Krai has instead arrived in pieces. The practical effect
for a player is that the map marker count keeps moving — the region you finished exploring in 6.0 is
not the region on your screen now.

## Why it matters for returning players

This is the first arc where "I have already explored that region" is an unreliable statement. For
anyone coming back after a year away, the useful check is not which regions you have visited but
which version the map data on your account was last complete for. Nod-Krai rewards a re-sweep in a
way Inazuma or Sumeru did not.

## The rhythm underneath is unchanged

Roughly six weeks per version, limited character banners, seasonal events on the familiar calendar.
Nothing in the 6.x cycle has altered the cadence — only what gets delivered inside it. Lantern Rite
ran on schedule during the cycle, as it has every year.
