Rockstar has confirmed how fishing and hunting work in *Grand Theft Auto VI*, and the fishing half
is not what people who played *Red Dead Redemption II* will be expecting. **Rockstar North**'s
**Rob Nelson** told YouTuber **TGG** that fishing is strictly catch and release — you cannot keep
anything you pull out of the water. **GameSpot** reported the exchange on September 3.

Asked why, Nelson's answer was short: it "didn't fit this game." Fishing is there mainly for its
own sake, with fishing-related challenges and collectibles attached.

## Why that is a defensible call

*Red Dead Redemption II* let you catch a wide range of fish and then cook and eat them, and that
made sense for 1899, where a caught fish was dinner and the nearest alternative was a day's ride.

*GTA 6* is set now. Jason and Lucia live in a world of restaurants, drive-throughs and convenience
stores. Eating in *GTA 6* has a system attached — food makes you **gain weight**, and the surplus
calories can be turned into muscle at the **gym** — so the loop that a caught fish would have fed
into already exists and already has better inputs.

Cutting it means one less system pretending to matter. It also means fishing in a Rockstar game is
now, for the first time in a while, just fishing.

## Hunting is the one that pays

Hunting is treated differently. Nelson described it as one of the game's **many ways to make
money**, which puts it in a category fishing explicitly is not in.

It is also gated. The hunting system unlocks through **a friend of Jason's**, and TGG's theory is
that this character has not appeared in any *GTA 6* footage released so far. If that holds, it is
a small piece of evidence about how much of the cast Rockstar is still holding back six weeks out.

## Inspecting, and the dogs

Both systems borrow the **inspect** mechanic from *Red Dead Redemption II* — you can examine fish
and animals up close rather than only harvesting them.

That extends to dogs, which you can pet, inspect, and, per GameSpot's aside, scold. We would
prefer you didn't.

*GTA 6* launches **November 19** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Sony and Rockstar announced a
*GTA 6*-themed DualSense controller at the September 3 State of Play.
