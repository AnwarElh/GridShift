ZZZ reached its version 3.1 anniversary update having settled into the same roughly six-week rhythm
its stablemates run on. For anyone picking it up now, the useful distinction in what arrived is
between what stays and what does not.

## Enigma of the Labyrinth is permanent

The mode added at the anniversary — Enigma of the Labyrinth: Operation Bagel — is a permanent
addition rather than a limited event. That matters for a new player deciding where to spend a first
week: permanent content is where your account progress compounds, and time spent learning it is not
wasted when the patch turns over.

## The six-week rhythm is the planning unit

HoYoverse has held to roughly six weeks per version. Whatever you are saving toward, that is the
cadence to plan against — banners, event windows and the content refresh all track it.

## The crossover is coming, but not yet

A crossover with Honkai: Star Rail is confirmed for winter 2026. HoYoverse has not put an exact date
or full scope on it. If you are returning specifically for that, you have time; if you are starting
now, you will be comfortably established before it lands.

## Platforms

Windows, iOS, Android and PS5 since July 2024, with Xbox Series added in June 2025. Account progress
carries across them, so the platform question is about where you prefer to play rather than where you
have to commit.
