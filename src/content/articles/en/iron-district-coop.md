Ninefold confirmed on Tuesday the four-player co-operative mode it had long referred to without
a date. It arrives "this winter", free, and with no premium currency — the studio committed to
that in writing.

The open question is pacing: Iron District is turn-based, and no demonstration has yet shown how
four players share a single turn.
